About me

Hi, I'm Sunshine, a web designer at junior-plus level. I got into design while building a poster for my college org's flower sale, and I haven't stopped since.

Over the last two years I've designed landing pages, booking flows and product sites for small local brands. I like interfaces that feel quiet and obvious, where a visitor never has to wonder what to tap next.

Right now I'm sharpening my UX research and prototyping, and I'm looking for a team where I can learn from senior designers and ship real work.

UX/UI

Glow Atelier: booking in three taps

Problem. A small beauty studio took every booking through Instagram messages. Staff spent hours a day replying, and clients waited for answers or gave up.

Solution. I mapped how clients actually booked, cut the flow to service, time and confirmation, and designed a mobile-first booking page with clear prices and a reminder step.

Result. In the prototype tests, five of five clients booked without help in under a minute. The studio's owner said it removed most of her daily message backlog.

Role: UX/UI designer. Tools: Figma, Tilda. Time: 3 weeks.

How I work

  1. Discover

    I ask about goals, audience and what already works, then collect references.

  2. Wireframe

    I sketch structure and flows before choosing any colors or fonts.

  3. Design

    I build the style, components and a clickable prototype in Figma.

  4. Hand off

    I deliver clean files and stay available while the site goes live.
